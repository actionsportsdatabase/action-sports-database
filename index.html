Before search bars

The first ASDB was a stack of magazines and a fax machine.

As a kid, founder Adam Wright sat in his dad’s office searching action-sports magazines for fax numbers. When he found one, he sent the brand a cover sheet, his résumé and surf photos. ASDB turns that same instinct—find the people, make the connection, preserve the story—into a living archive.
